  Level 4: watery stool outside clothes

---

DEFINITIONS (apply strictly):

- "confirmed_start": The note contains clear evidence the patient HAS taken or IS actively taking Daybue (e.g., "started Daybue", "on Daybue", "taking trofinetide", "dose increased", side effects or improvements reported while on Daybue, titration language).
- "confirmed_end": The note contains clear evidence the patient STOPPED Daybue (e.g., "discontinued", "stopped Daybue", "no longer on trofinetide", "Daybue was stopped due to...").
